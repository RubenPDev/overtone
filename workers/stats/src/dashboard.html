People per day

Browsers that used the site each day (UTC, when puzzles change), split by whether they played. Only browsers that touched a page count, so crawlers and link previews are left out.

Players per game

Browsers that started at least one attempt of each game, per day. Someone playing two games counts in both.

First-time funnel

Browsers new in this period, and how far each got. The biggest drop is where to look.

How often players play

Days each player played in this period.

Returns by first day

New players grouped by the day they first played, and the share of them who came back on each later day. Blank where that day hasn't happened yet.

The games

Attempts and solves in this period. Hover a heading for what it means.

Cadence puzzles

Per tune, newest first. Solves count each player's first solve; replays after it are left out.

Facet puzzles

Moves over par is the median for players who solved it. A restart part way through counts as giving up an attempt.

Junctions puzzles

Cleared is 90% of the traffic through; perfect is that with no street going red. A run is one attempt.

Lessons

Browsers that finished or skipped each game's how-to-play, and at which step they skipped.

Shares and likes

Browsers that shared a result or liked a game.
